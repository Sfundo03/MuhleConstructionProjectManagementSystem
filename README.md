MuhleOps is a role-based construction management platform built for Muhle Building that replaces spreadsheets and WhatsApp chaos with a single source of truth. It digitises the full project lifecycle — from hiring and client onboarding to equipment maintenance, payroll, and project handover — with real-time visibility, audit trails, and end-to-end traceability across multiple construction sites.

Key capabilities:

📋 Recruitment, client onboarding & contract signing

🗓️ Duty rosters with certification & conflict validation

🚜 Equipment allocation, damage reporting & repair lifecycle

⏱️ QR-code clock in/out with geofencing & offline support

🔍 Quality inspections & variation management

💰 Payroll with anomaly detection & payslip generation

✍️ Digital client sign-off & project closure

👥 User Roles
Admin · Client · Project Manager · Site Manager · Finance Officer · Crew Member
